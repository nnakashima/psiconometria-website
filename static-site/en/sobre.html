Psiconometria manifesto

People are not mechanical assets, rankings or mere productive resources. Psiconometria exists so that organisations treat human factors with the same rigour with which they treat numbers — and treat numbers about people with the same care with which they treat people.

Understand people without reducing people to numbers. Turn human factors into measurable hypotheses — and hypotheses into verifiable decisions, with quantitative rigour, context and ethics.

Collective learning: the asset that no dashboard captures on its own.
